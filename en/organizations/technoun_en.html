TechnoUN

Official name: TechnoUN

Type: scientific and technological company

Description:

A technology company founded in 2038 by Eichlandian scientific figures in Comsul.

It is known that the company maintained moderate success for several years. However, following the emergence of information indicating that TechnoUN conducted experiments on humans[1] and tested chemical compounds and cybernetic implants on them, it rapidly ceased its operations.

1. Comsul Government Research AK-7751

It is assumed that the company was shut down in 2041 by the government due to illegal activities[2] (the official explanation states bankruptcy).[3]

2. Documents NB-8819-U56 and NB-9916-C19

3. Extracts from Comsul news archives from 2047 (notably the news channel “Daily Comsul”)

Information regarding the subsequent fate of the company’s employees and test subjects remains unknown to Kansan authorities.

Key Characteristics:

  • private scientific and technological company with an undefined level of state oversight
  • founded by Eichlandian scientific emigrants (2038)
  • application of restricted or ethically prohibited experimental methods
  • lack of transparent public reporting on funding and research activities
  • rapid shutdown following the initiation of governmental investigations
  • contradiction between the official explanation for closure and unofficial data
  • partial loss / fragmentation of archival records after 2041
