Additional Information:

Attack on the Ramen Restaurant

On July 23, 2062, the "Furusato" ramen restaurant was attacked, resulting in significant damage to the establishment's building. A subsequent investigation and analysis of statements made by the individuals involved established the involvement of representatives of Uncle Jin's management in organizing the attack.

According to the attackers, the attack was carried out with the intention of intimidating the employees and subsequently forcing the establishment to close. Employees of "Furusato" later confirmed that the owner of the ramen restaurant had repeatedly received offers to purchase the building from the CEO of Uncle Jin's, which eventually escalated into threats.

"Uncle Jin's" denied any involvement in organizing the attack, but agreed to fully cover the medical expenses of those injured and the costs of restoring the establishment. Following the resolution of the case, the chain continued its operations as usual.
