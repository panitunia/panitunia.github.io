Blue Sky

Official name: Blue Sky

Type (according to NRC-3 classification): semi-state humanitarian organization

Category: civil stabilization and disaster recovery

Officially declared mission: assisting the citizenry in resolving crisis situations

Influence:

“Blue Sky” ensures a reduction in the level of social losses through the rehabilitation of the population after crisis situations,[1] in particular socially unstable population groups such as refugees, former gang members, and cult affiliates.

1. Documents BL-SK12 and BL-SK57

They also frequently add new data regarding illegal or closed organizations, information about which is often difficult to obtain.[2]

2. Data archive No. B4912

Recorded key characteristics:

“Blue Sky” regularly analyzes the social condition of the country in order to forecast potential crisis situations and social destabilizations.

Social Status:

The overall reputation of “Blue Sky” among the population is fairly positive (63% positive and 26% neutral responses).[3]

According to observations, the existence of a humanitarian organization capable of eliminating the consequences of disasters provides a sense of calm to the majority of the surveyed population.[3]

3. Survey report No. 66327

It has also been noted that some large corporations, such as Kuroborus and Cybertech, may publicly disclose financial contributions to “Blue Sky” in order to improve their social image.
