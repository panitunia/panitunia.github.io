Type of organization (classification according to KK-7): energy production

Officially declared goal:

Chronology of operations:

Founded in 2036 as a project aimed at developing a new source of energy. It is known that in the early stages of its existence, the company did not achieve the desired success.[1][2]

During the political and economic unrest in Kanzan resulting from the crisis (see WA-1828-J91), the popularity of Kuroborus increased significantly, eventually turning it into a market leader.

The influence of Kuroborus began to grow rapidly, gradually extending into other sectors of the economy.

It is known that Kuroborus now exerts indirect influence over other major corporations in the country.[3]
