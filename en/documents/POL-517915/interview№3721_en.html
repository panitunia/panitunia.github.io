Interview No. 3718 (Incident witness interview).

Interviewee: Yui Takahashi

Interviewer: ■■■ ■■■

Date: 23.07.2062

Location: Akako, Kifu City, Garden District, Apt. 12, Bldg. 42 (Kifu Police Station No. 5)

— First, please state your full name and date of birth.

Y: Yui Takahashi. Born September 18, 2038.

— Did you witness the beginning of the incident?

Y: No... my friends and I... um... we left a bar and heard some noise coming from a nearby ramen shop.

— What happened next?

Y: We... well... decided to check if anyone needed our help... so we went to see what was going on.

— Go on.

Y: When we got closer to the ramen shop, there was a loud crash and... I think something inside the shop caught fire?... There was a flash.

— What did you see after that?

Y: There... there was a fire... and people started running out of the ramen shop.

— Were you able to make out who these people were?

Y: No... I think they were men wearing dark clothes. I didn't get a good look at them... As far as I remember, there were two of them.

— Do you remember which direction they ran?

Y: Toward the Upper District? They ran in the opposite direction from us and disappeared around the corner.

— Is there anything else you'd like to add?

Y: No... I just... hope everyone's alive. We didn't know what to do... we were afraid it might be someone from a gang... so we just called the fire department.

— All right. We'll end it here.

/End of interview./
