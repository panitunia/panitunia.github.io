Incident Description:

At approximately 23:32, the central police station in Kifu City received a report of a fire at a local ramen shop.

By the time the police arrived at 23:43, emergency services were already working to extinguish the fire. Two restaurant employees (Y. Li and K. Kononenko) were observed rescuing valuables from the premises. Three other individuals were also outside the building in critical condition and were receiving emergency medical attention. One of them was the shop employee A. Hino.

Following the extinguishing of the fire at the scene, as well as later at the police station, all three restaurant employees, three men subsequently identified in the course of the investigation as the instigators of the attack, and seven witnesses were interviewed. Testimonies from two of the witnesses were used to reconstruct the chronology of events.

Based on the analysis of testimonies provided by the participants and witnesses of the incident, the following sequence of events was established:

At approximately 23:06, five unidentified men forcibly entered the "Furusato" ramen shop, which was closed at the time.

They were confronted by one of the shop employee (Y. Li), who, upon noticing the unidentified individuals, contacted K. Kononenko, who later arrived at the scene together with A. Hino.[2].

A verbal altercation broke out between the parties, which subsequently escalated into a physical confrontation. During the altercation, the instigators of the attack also attempted to destroy the shop's interior.[3][4][5][6]. As a result, the electrical supply system of the establishment was damaged, causing the fire to break out.

As a result of the incident, four individuals sustained second-degree burns, while two required emergency medical assistance. Additionally, all participants in the incident sustained physical injuries of varying severity, ranging from minor injuries to fractures.
