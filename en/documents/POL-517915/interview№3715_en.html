ОInterviewee: Yoshio Lee

Interviewer: ■■■ ■■■

Date: 23.07.2062

Location: Akako, Kifu City, Garden District, Apt. 12, Bldg. 42 (Kifu Police Station No. 5)

— First, state your full name and date of birth.

Y: Yoshio Lee. December 1, 2034.

— Please recount the events in chronological order, starting from the beginning.

Y: I was checking some documents in the office after my shift...

— Are you responsible for maintaining the records?

Y: Well, of course not! But someone's gotta take care of it while the owner is away... Anyway, I was just about to head home when I heard some kind of noise outside. I looked out of the office, and there was a group of men with baseball bats in the dining area!

— Did you recognize any of the attackers?

Y: Never seen them before! And it was dark as hell in there, too. Couldn't see shit! They came straight at me, too! I locked myself in the office and started calling Khrystyna.

— Why didn't you call the police immediately?

Y: Five fucking brutes were trying to break down my door! I just dialed the first number that came to mind. You think I had time to sit around and think?... Ahem. Sorry...

— Please continue.

Y: Then I heard Aki's voice. He was arguing with those assholes. I figured that was my chance: I flung the door open and whacked the nearest one with a lamp I'd grabbed from the office. Aki and Khrystyna were already there, too. Then the fight started. Actually, calling it a fight would be an exaggeration! They were just smashing everything in sight! The entire dining area is a complete mess now!

— Describe the details of the altercation. Did you attempt to—

Y: I was trying not to get myself killed and defend myself however I could! I fought them off with whatever I could get my hands on! God, I have no idea how we even survived that! It would've been hard enough to handle even one of them one-on-one, and there were more of them than us!

— How did the fire start?

Y: I don't know! Looks like one of those assholes damaged some equipment in the kitchen, and it caught fire. Then something exploded. I was grabbing everything I could see. Documents, the cash register, equipment, whatever else I could carry out. Khrystyna was doing the same... God, the kitchen's completely destroyed now! It's the heart of the ramen shop! How are we supposed to keep working? And what will Mr. Okada say? He poured his heart and soul into this ramen shop!

— Is there anything else you'd like to add?

Y: Yes. I demand that whoever did this pay for everything! They should at the very least cover all the repair costs and compensate us for emotional distress! How dare they do something like this?! I—

— Thank you. We'll conclude the interview here.
