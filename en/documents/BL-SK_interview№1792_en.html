Interview No. 1792 (Survey of a former member of the closed religious organization “Church of Galaxia”)

Interviewee: Kaori Haruno

Interviewer: ■■■ ■■■

Date: 18.05.2049

Location: Okuzawa City, ■■■ Street, Building No. ■■, “Blue Sky” Facility

— To begin with, please state your name and age.

K: My name is Kaori Haruno. I’m 17…

— Please state your current location.

K: Right now?... The “Blue Sky” building… Okuzawa City… Um… I don’t know the exact address.

— How did you learn about the religious organization you were part of?

K: After my dad was imprisoned… my mom later started taking us to their sermons. They helped us with money and food, so my mom decided they were good people and wanted to join them.

— As you mentioned earlier, your father was imprisoned due to illegal activity?

K: I don’t know the details, but yes. We were struggling financially, so he tried to feed us however he could.

— As far as we know, this religious organization calls itself the “Church of Galaxia.” Is that correct?

К: Yes, that’s what they call themselves. They said we are all part of Galaxia… like cells of one body…

— How did your initiation into the church take place? Were there any special rituals?

K: I don’t remember very well. My sister and I officially joined the church after our mother died, so I was too devastated to pay attention to what was happening around me. I remember being forced to eat a boiled egg with raw liver… gghh… They also gave us amulets said to protect us from misfortune.

— Did the church use any terms different from ordinary language?

K: Uhh… no, I don’t think so? Even though they assign different meanings to some things, they use the same words…

— What do you mean by “different meanings”?

K: Well… the Church believes that Galaxia… the universe, is a living being, and that humans are its cells. Or that stars are the souls of ancestors…

— Do you believe this?

K: I don’t know…

— What was your daily routine while living in the Church?

K: I would wake up, get dressed, and go to the Church school. After classes, I had to assist our priest as a novice…

— “Novice”?

K: Yes. It’s… um… something like a priest’s trainee? We were supposed to become priests after that.

— I see. Please continue. What exactly did you do?

K: I learned how to perform rituals and did various chores like cleaning, restocking incense supplies, or doing laundry. That usually took most of the day, but I still had a few free hours which I spent with my sister or alone.

— Were there any other required activities?

K: Well, we sometimes listened to sermons and meditated to connect with Galaxia. Sermons were… a couple of times a week. For regular believers, it was once a week. And meditations were held every full moon.

— Were these activities voluntary or mandatory?

K: Voluntary… though in practice, mandatory. If you missed sermons too often, the priest would start asking questions. I was punished a few times for missing them.

— How were you punished?

K: Well… usually for missing sermons, the punishment was silence. No one would speak to me until I went to the priest and repented.

— How were rules enforced?

K: Usually someone older was around most of the time, or someone else would report it. Sometimes people felt so guilty they confessed on their own.

— The Church of Galaxia is a closed organization, correct? When and how did you last have contact with the outside world?

K: Yes, it is quite closed. They said contact with outsiders contaminates us… I… um… I usually only went out accompanied by an elder, and I was forbidden from speaking with outsiders… As for the last time… probably before my mother died? Although I did run away a couple of times just to walk around… but I didn’t talk to anyone…

— At what point did you realize you needed to leave?

K: I wouldn’t say there was a single moment… it was more like doubts gradually built up… They punished anyone who asked too many questions or broke rules… I felt like I was constantly being watched. When I was outside the Church, I felt much more serene…

— Did you talk about this with your sister?

K: Yes, I brought it up several times. I even suggested escaping together a few times, but she refused… She believes the Church is right and that we must maintain harmony for Galaxia, even if it’s difficult.

— I see. We will end here for today.

/End of interview/
