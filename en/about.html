[ENTRY FROM A TOURIST GUIDE: unofficial document]

WORLD

Our planet is divided into eight regions.

After the Winter Ash — a massive nuclear war — only three regions remain habitable: Penglai, Shavgar, and Garampaani.

Although some brave souls still explore the others, we strongly advise against it. Some things are better left untouched.

KANZAN

We live in Penglai, in the country of Kanzan — a center of technology and energy. Kanzan takes great pride in its scientific achievements, so it is customary here to show respect for technology and those who create it.

But remember: sometimes behind the shine of innovation, there is more than meets the eye.

KUROBORUS

Kuroborus is one of the largest corporations of Kanzan, which is often referred to as a beacon of stability. It made a huge contribution to the country's economy during the crisis following the Winter Ash and invented a breakthrough method of energy extraction.

Is it true that it actually controls the government? No, these are just rumors... at least, so they say.
